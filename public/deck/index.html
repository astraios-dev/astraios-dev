02.Problem

Retail traders are drowning in data and starved of signal.

Brokerages give customers a feed, not an edge. Bloomberg-grade intelligence is priced for institutions. Alpha-generation tools aimed at retail are either black-box auto-traders with no explainability, or indicator soup with no execution path.

  • No single surface ranks trades by confidence and reasoning.
  • No paper-first path from signal to live capital.
  • No API that a developer or small fund can actually build on.
03.Solution

One engine, two surfaces.

A shared strategy engine reads market microstructure, macro regime, and filings data. The consumer app renders it as ranked trades with confidence scores and explanations. The API exposes the same signals to developers, routed through Alpaca paper accounts by default.

Consumer App

Read the trade before taking it.

Ranked signals, confidence scores, and an explanation of why each call was surfaced — so users build trust in the model, not blind faith.

Trading API

Paper-first auto-execution.

REST for strategy state, WebSocket for live signals. Paper accounts by default, live routing opt-in, kill switch per account.

04.Architecture

Four layers from raw ticks to routed order.

01

Data Layer

Polygon and CCXT for prices, FRED for macro, SEC EDGAR for filings — normalised into TimescaleDB, streamed over Kafka.

02

Model Layer

HMM + LSTM ensembles for regime detection, Temporal Fusion Transformers for multi-horizon forecasts, PPO/SAC for portfolios.

03

Strategy Engine

Signal generation with confidence scores, fractional Kelly position sizing, VectorBT for thousands of sweeps per minute.

04

Execution Layer

Alpaca paper accounts by default. Live routing opt-in, Redis-queued between strategy and orders, end-to-end logged.

05.Why Now

Three windows just opened at the same time.

  • Retail is durable. Millions of self-directed accounts stayed after the 2020–2024 wave; brokerage tooling has not caught up.
  • Models got cheap. TFTs, open-weight LLMs, and GPU rental make research-grade inference runnable on a single node.
  • APIs are plumbed. Alpaca, CCXT, and Polygon mean a pre-seed team can ship a real execution loop in weeks, not quarters.

We enter through the first bucket and compound into the second. Retail is a top-of-funnel for developer and fund users — the same engine, priced differently.

07.Research Protocol

From raw ticks to live capital, in four disciplined moves.

  1. 01

    Ingest

    One store of truth for prices, macro, and filings. Training, backtests, and inference all read from it.

  2. 02

    Model

    Baseline first. Ensembles and RL only earn their way in once they fairly beat the simpler model.

  3. 03

    Backtest

    Strict time splits, survivorship-adjusted universes, and look-ahead checks before paper trading opens.

  4. 04

    Deploy

    Thirty days paper, ONNX inference path, and a per-account kill switch wired in from day one.

08.Business Model

Subscription for humans, usage for code.

A

Consumer

Free tier: delayed signals on paper. Paid tier ($19–49/mo) unlocks real-time ranked trades, explanations, and live-trade hand-off.

B

Developer API

Metered per call and per live account. Paper is free; live routing bills per-execution plus a small AUM-linked take.

C

Data Flywheel

Every accept/reject, hold time, and override is a training label. The data we own, not the model we run, becomes the moat.
